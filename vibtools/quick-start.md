# Quick Start Guide for VibTools

Welcome to VibTools! This guide is written specifically for **everyday users and creators**. You do not need any coding knowledge, terminal commands, or technical background to use VibTools.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started in 3 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Open the VibTools Portal

Launch the VibTools application on your computer or open your team's VibTools catalog in any web browser.

You will see a visual dashboard displaying all available tools, workspace setups, and utilities.

### Step 2: Choose Your Tool or Workspace Preset

Browse through the cards on your screen. Each tool displays:

- A friendly name and description
- Clear screenshots showing what it looks like
- Tags showing whether it is free, open-source, and ready to use

Click on any tool (such as **Session Manager Pro** or **Workspace Automator**) to view details.

### Step 3: Launch with One Click

Click the big blue **"Start"** or **"Launch"** button.

That is it! VibTools automatically handles everything in the background:

- No complicated setup wizards
- No environment variables to configure
- No command prompt or terminal windows

---

## 💡 Top Tips for Beginners

1. **Use Presets**: If you often work with multiple windows or monitors, save your layout as a **Workspace Preset**. You can restore your entire workspace with a single click tomorrow morning.
2. **Safe to Experiment**: VibTools does not modify your system files. You can safely try any tool or preset and turn it off whenever you want.
3. **Automatic Saving**: Your preferences, layouts, and settings are saved automatically on your computer.
