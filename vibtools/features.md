# VibTools Features & Overview

VibTools is built to make productivity effortless for everyone—whether you are a student, designer, office professional, or developer.

Here is a simple, non-technical tour of what VibTools can do for you.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 1-Click Catalog Import

Instead of filling out long forms when adding a project, VibTools reads a lightweight configuration file (`vibtools.json`). With a single click, product details, logos, social preview banners, and download links load automatically into your dashboard.

### 2. Social Media Preview Cards (Open Graph OG Images)

When you share a VibTools link on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Discord, or Facebook, it automatically displays a high-resolution branded preview card (`1200x630`) with clean typography and badges.

### 3. Visual Workspace Manager

- Arrange your favorite apps and browser tabs in seconds.
- Save multiple setups: e.g. "Deep Work Setup", "Video Meeting Setup", and "Creative Studio Setup".
- Restore your exact multi-screen layout anytime with one click.

### 4. Zero-Code Automation

- Automate window positioning and daily startup apps without writing scripts or code.
- Simple, friendly toggle switches and visual buttons for all settings.

### 5. 100% Local & Private

- Your data stays on your personal device.
- No tracking cookies, no advertising telemetry, and no mandatory cloud accounts.
- Works offline whenever you do not have internet access.

### 6. Beautiful Dark & Light Modes

- Crisp, modern design that is gentle on your eyes.
- Automatically matches your operating system theme or lets you choose your favorite look.
