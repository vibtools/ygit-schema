# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Got questions? Here are quick, jargon-free answers to the most common questions about VibTools.

---

### Q1: Do I need programming or tech skills to use VibTools?

**No, absolutely not.** VibTools is designed with simple visual buttons, switches, and clear instructions. You will never need to open a command terminal, write code, or edit system configurations.

---

### Q2: Is VibTools free to use?

**Yes.** VibTools is completely free and open-source. There are no hidden subscription fees, paywalls, or feature locks.

---

### Q3: Where are my files and settings saved?

All your preferences and workspace presets are stored locally on your own computer. VibTools does not upload your files, work habits, or private documents to any remote server.

---

### Q4: What is the `vibtools.json` file?

`vibtools.json` is a small text profile for a tool. Think of it like a business card or product tag: it tells catalog websites the name of the tool, what it does, what logo and social images to display, and where to download it.

---

### Q5: What are Social OG images?

"OG" stands for Open Graph. When you share a web link on social networks (like Twitter, LinkedIn, Slack, or Discord), the platform fetches an image to display alongside the title. VibTools provides built-in support for high-quality `1200x630` social cards so your links look polished and professional.

---

### Q6: How do I update to newer versions?

VibTools checks for updates safely and notifies you when a new release is available. You can update with a single click from the app, or download the latest release installer from GitHub.

---

### Q7: What operating systems are supported?

VibTools supports modern 64-bit systems including:

- Windows 10 and Windows 11
- macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- Linux (modern desktop distributions)
